I visited Hanoi, the capital of Vietnam.

<!--more-->

- Halong Bay

The northern region of Vietnam in winter is (surprisingly) very cold, so I recommend dressing warmly.
I was happy to enjoy plenty of pho (Vietnamese rice noodles).
