## Animal Crossing

This was my first Switch game during the COVID-19 era.
It's a game where you fish with villagers, drink coffee, watch fireworks, see shooting stars, and make snowmen. Isn't it really cute?
Animal Crossing is a very calm game in all aspects - simple controls, background music, story, etc. - compared to other games.
A game where you build your own house on a deserted island, become self-sufficient, and peacefully stroll with animal friends... Isn't it really romantic? But in reality:
- Occasional debt collection letters telling you to pay back money quickly
- House ranking evaluations saying your house is dirty
- Getting stung by a scorpion while trying to catch it for money and ending up unconscious
- Precious NPCs' famous quotes (saying "I'll do it tomorrow" again tomorrow)
- Getting stung by a bee while collecting branches to make money and ending up unconscious
- Paying a fortune for what turns out to be a fake artwork
- Losing your principal while trying to make money with turnip stocks
- Catching cockroaches that appear when you return home after a long time
- And so on...

It feels like the game is slapping you on the back of the head saying "That's enough, get out into reality now." Is this Nintendo's grand plan to prevent game addiction? But these elements seem to make the game more fun by giving you a sense of responsibility.

Cute 3D characters and motions, calm BGM, an island you can decorate as you please...
I hope you can feel the happiness of ending your day while listening to the encouraging messages, good quotes, nicknames, and songs from your island residents.
