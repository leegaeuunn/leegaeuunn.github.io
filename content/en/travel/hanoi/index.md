## 🇻🇳 Vietnam

I visited Hanoi, the capital of Vietnam. I was very, very happy to be able to eat pho to my heart's content.
Do you know why lime comes with pho? It's for disinfecting chopsticks with lime juice~ Please keep this in mind.. 🍋‍🟩🥢
The northern region of Vietnam in winter is (surprisingly) very cold, so I recommend dressing warmly.
