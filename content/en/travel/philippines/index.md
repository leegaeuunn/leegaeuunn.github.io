## 🥭 Philippines

I went to Cebu, Philippines for language study abroad.
I'm not sure about my English improvement... but my mango cutting skills have improved!!
It was wonderful to talk with teachers every day and meet people of different nationalities and communicate in English.
On weekdays, I chatted in English, and on weekends, I swam in the sea....🏊🏻‍♂️
I received birthday congratulations from Chinese, Japanese, Taiwanese, Hong Kong, and Filipino friends, and shared cakes together.
It was a dream-like month.
I ♥  CEBU
